Privacy and terms

Last updated 18 November 2026

What this is

DNS Check is a free tool that shows which DNS resolvers your device is using, so you can see whether you're using Control D and whether anything else is answering. It's run by Ol775 as a personal project, with no accounts and no adverts. It isn't affiliated with Control D.

What this site keeps

  • Nothing about you in a database. The site has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't store test results.
  • One cookie, dnsck. After you pass the human check it records that you passed, so you aren't asked again for an hour. It holds an expiry time and a signature, no personal data. It's strictly necessary, HttpOnly, and expires after one hour.
  • Your browser may keep ordinary cached copies of the page, like any website.

Who else sees your data

The test works by your browser contacting other services. Each sees your IP address as part of making the request, and the ones that need more are listed here:

The Connection tab also reads what Cloudflare's network reports about your request (your IP, network, country, city and timezone) and compares your IP with the public Tor exit list, which the Worker downloads; no visitor data is sent to the Tor Project. The Ports tab makes the Worker open TCP connections from Cloudflare's network to your own IP address only; it never accepts a target from the request. Nothing from these checks is stored.

I don't control these services, and what they do with your data is covered by their own policies.

Legal basis and your rights

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, the basis is legitimate interests: running a diagnostic tool you chose to use and keeping it free of bots. Because this site doesn't store personal data itself, there's nothing for me to export or delete, but you can ask. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
